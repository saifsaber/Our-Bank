# Offer Catalog

Pricing is set at **entry-tier** deliberately: the first three sales buy proof, not profit.
Raise prices after 3 delivered jobs with testimonials (see `ops/pricing-ladder.md`).

Rate anchor: BIM bureaus bill clients $25–55/hr for MEP work. Entry pricing below sits under
that anchor while still being 3–8× typical Egyptian salaried hourly rates.

---

## O1 — Distribution Board / Panel Schedule Production ★ FLAGSHIP
| | |
|---|---|
| **Target** | MEP consultants, electrical contractors, BIM bureaus, design-build firms |
| **Problem** | A junior engineer burns 4–8 hours per board building schedules by hand in Excel, then transcribes them into Revit. Errors in cable size and voltage drop surface at submittal review and cost a revision cycle. |
| **Solution** | Send the load list in any format. Back comes a phase-balanced board schedule with cable sizing, protective device selection, voltage-drop verification, and a QA exception list — plus a CSV that imports straight into the Revit panel schedule. |
| **Deliverable** | Submittal-ready PDF schedule + Excel + Revit-import CSV + QA exception list |
| **Delivery** | 24–48 h for up to 5 boards |
| **Entry price** | **$60 / board**, or $250 for 5 boards |
| **Why they buy** | Replaces a full day of engineer time for the price of an hour, and catches errors before the reviewer does. |
| **Upsell** | Full project distribution schedule; single-line diagram; Revit model integration |
| **Recurring** | Per-project retainer — every project has 10–60 boards |
| **Demo** | The live generator — the customer can run their own numbers before paying |

## The wedge: what makes these sellable by an unknown seller
Every offer above leads with something the buyer can verify **before** paying:
a free single-sheet QA review, a free 20-page conversion, or the live generator
they can run their own numbers through. Nobody has to trust a stranger — they
just have to check the output.
