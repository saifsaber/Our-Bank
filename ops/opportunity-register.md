## Market facts established this cycle
- Freelance MEP BIM modeller benchmark **$40.33/hr** US average; Revit MEP designer **$47.71/hr** average, $24–62 range.
- BIM bureaus bill MEP outsourcing at **$25–55/hr** to end clients.
- Fiverr Revit MEP gigs run **$15 → $200**; the floor is commodity modelling, the ceiling is coordination and clash detection.
- In-house US electrical drafting costs a firm **$480k–720k/yr** in salaries — the arbitrage that funds all outsourcing.
- Outsourcing firms report **30–60% production cost reduction** — this is the number to quote in outreach.
- Demand drivers named repeatedly for 2026: AI in design automation, BIM mandates, 3D laser scanning.
