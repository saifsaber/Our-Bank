Reference data note. Current-carrying capacities and voltage-drop coefficients are the published tabulated values for copper conductors under the stated installation method and ambient temperature (IEC 60364-5-52 / BS 7671 Appendix 4; NEC Table 310.16). They are a calculation aid for design development — every schedule must be verified by the responsible engineer against the project specification, the authority having jurisdiction, and the manufacturer's device data before issue for construction.
