# Outreach Messages

**Rules that are not negotiable**
- Every message is personally adapted. No identical mass sends.
- Never claim experience, clients, or credentials Saif does not have.
- Never invent testimonials or a company that does not exist.
- Lead with *their* problem, not his CV.
- One clear, low-friction call to action. Never two.
- If no reply after two follow-ups, stop. That contact is closed.

**Structure used throughout:** PROBLEM → OBSERVATION → SOLUTION → PROOF → SIMPLE CTA

**The proof link** is the live Distribution Board Schedule Generator. It does the work of
a portfolio because the recipient can put their own numbers in and watch it produce a
schedule. Send it in the *first* message — it is what makes a stranger credible.
